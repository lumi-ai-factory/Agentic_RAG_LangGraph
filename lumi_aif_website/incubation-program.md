# Venture Incubation program

By LUMI AI Factory x 28DIGITAL

## Call for startups!

A free, 12-week online acceleration program for early-stage AI startups that need high-performance computing to grow. Get access to LUMI, technical and business mentoring, and a stage at the in-person Demo Day in Finland.

[Apply by 11 January 2027](https://www.f6s.com/lumi-ai-factory-x-28digital/apply)

[Join the first info session 30 nov](https://events.teams.microsoft.com/event/f76fa151-afcc-4f4e-b3c4-7bcdac7ad8f1@7c3574d4-f2c2-4447-bd77-d3c3cc68be6e)

## Why apply? Supercomputing access and investor readiness in one pathway

#### **Compute resources**

Access to LUMI’s supercomputing capacity to train and scale AI models.

#### **Expert support**

Tailored mentoring from technical and business experts.

#### **Visibility**

Pitch to investors and European stakeholders at the Demo Day.

#### **Network**

Join a community of founders and experts.

#### **No cost**

Free of charge and no equity required.

## Who should apply?

Early-stage startups with a clearly defined AI product, service or development project, and a team able to take it to market.

* A working prototype, existing AI solution or technically credible development plan
* A team covering both the technical and business domains
* A genuine need for LUMI’s compute resources
* Ready to commit actively to the full program

Company incorporated in Belgium, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, Iceland, Latvia, Norway, Poland, and Switzerland.

## How to apply?

One application per company, in English, via F6S. Create an account or log in, complete the form and upload:

* Completed F65 application form
* Certificate of company registration
* Pitch deck
* CVs of all team members

Incomplete or late applications, or those sent by other means such as email, are not accepted. Submit early to avoid technical issues.
