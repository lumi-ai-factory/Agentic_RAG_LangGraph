# AI Starter Pack

Your first steps in AI and supercomputing

Wondering what AI could do for your company? Have an idea involving AI, large amounts of data and high-performance computing, but are unsure where to begin?

AI Starter Pack helps start-ups and small and medium-sized enterprises take their first steps in AI development and supercomputing following a clear path that can be concluded **within three 3 months**. Our Starter Pack is available to [eligible companies in EU Member States and countries associated with the Horizon Europe programme](https://lumi-ai-factory.eu/pricing-and-eligibility/). Consultation, training and computing resources are available free of charge to eligible start-ups and SMEs, subject to the conditions of the relevant service and computing access scheme.

LUMI AI Factory experts can help you to:

* **Explore your opportunity:** assess your company’s AI readiness, clarify your business idea and evaluate its technical feasibility.
* **Identify what you need:** understand the skills, data and computing resources required, and find suitable training and support.
* **Build trust from the start:** identify relevant trustworthy AI considerations, such as data privacy, fairness and reliability, and learn which EU AI Act requirements may apply when your idea becomes a product. We provide initial guidance, not legal advice.
* **Start experimenting:** apply for suitable computing resources and receive technical guidance to develop and test a first proof of concept.
* **Plan your next steps:** review what you have learned, including what the EU AI Act will require as you move from prototype to product, and identify further support and computing resources as your project develops.
